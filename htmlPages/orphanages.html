Umuntu Ngabantu Alliance has identified several orphanagesin peril.

Umuntu Ngabantu Alliance has identified the increased gap between the number of orphaned children in South Africa and the number of orphanages that are able to support them. We have identified several orphanages that are in need of support and we are committed to helping them provide the necessary care and resources for these vulnerable children.

We also plan to expand the reach of these orphanages to the masses as there are a number of people in need who do not know where to turn to for assistance, which is where Umuntu Ngabantu Alliance comes in.
