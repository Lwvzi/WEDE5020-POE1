About Umuntu Ngabantu Alliance

Umuntu Ngabantu Alliance's was started with the goal of helping connect potential donors and volunteers with orphanages across South Africa that are in need of support. We believe that every child deserves a safe and loving home, and we are committed to making that a reality for as many children as possible.

Our Story.

Our journey began with a simple yet powerful idea: to bridge the gap between those who want to help and the orphanages that need support. Over the years, we plan to grow into a network of dedicated individuals and organizations working together to make a difference in the lives of orphaned children across South Africa.

Umuntu Ngabantu Alliance is a non-profit organization dedicated to improving the lives of orphaned children in South Africa. This is an ever-growing effort to make a positive impact in the communities we serve.
