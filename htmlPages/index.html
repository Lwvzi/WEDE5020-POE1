The Reason We Do What We Do.

South Africa has approximately has over 2.7 million children that are orphaned and in need of major support. Umuntu Ngabantu Alliance is committed to addressing this critical issue and providing the necessary resources and assistance to these vulnerable children.

Our Mission

To connect potential donors and volunteers with orphanages across South Africa that are in need of support.

Our Vision

To create a world where every orphaned child has access to the care, support, and opportunities they need to thrive.

Our Values

Compassion, Integrity, Collaboration, Empowerment, and Accountability.

2.7 million

The number of orphaned children in South Africa who are in need of support and care.

Highest Rates

Kwa-Zulu Natal, the Eastern Cape, and the Free State have the highest rates of orphaned children across South Africa.

Funding not Space

The main challenge facing orphanages in South Africa is the lack of funding, not a lack of space, space can be created by adding more assets which requires financial support.

How Umuntu Ngabantu Alliance Works

Umuntu Ngabantu Alliance works by connecting potential donors and volunteers with orphanages across South Africa that are in need of support. We facilitate this connection through our online platform and community outreach programs. We however do not run any orphanges ourselves.
